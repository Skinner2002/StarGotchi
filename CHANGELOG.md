# Changelog

## 1.0.0 (unreleased – waiting for hardware test)
- One bot with five life stages (Core, Mini, Junior, Astro/Combat/Scrap)
- Four stats with real-time decay, night sleep and charging dock
- Emergency shutdown after 8 h with two empty stats, reboot keeps the stage
- Tilt-controlled mini-game "Asteroid Run"
- Stats and setup pages (clock, brightness, sound, reset)
- Screen dims after 30 s and turns off after 60 s
- Save survives power loss and web-installer updates
- Browser installer on GitHub Pages
