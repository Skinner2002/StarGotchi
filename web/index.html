STARGOTCHI

A pocket bot that needs your care.

You need
  • Waveshare ESP32-S3-Touch-AMOLED-1.75 (with or without case)
  • A USB-C data cable
  • A computer with Chrome or Edge
Install
  1. Plug the board into your computer.
  2. Click Install StarGotchi and pick the serial port.
  3. Updating? Leave "Erase device" unticked – your bot stays alive.
  4. Wait until it says done, then unplug. Set the clock on first start.

Deutsch: Board per USB anschließen, auf den Knopf klicken, Port wählen. Bei Updates „Erase device“ NICHT anhaken, sonst ist dein Bot weg.

Your browser can't do this. Please use Chrome or Edge on a computer. Please open this page over https.
